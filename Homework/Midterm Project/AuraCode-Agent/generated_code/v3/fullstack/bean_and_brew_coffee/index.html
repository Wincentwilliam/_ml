Our Story

Founded in 2015, Bean & Brew started as a small cart on a rainy street corner. We believe that coffee is more than just a drink—it's a ritual, a moment of peace, and a bridge between people.

We source our beans ethically from sustainable farms in Ethiopia and Colombia, roasting them in small batches to ensure every cup is a masterpiece.
